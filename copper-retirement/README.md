# Copper Retirement Data: AT&T and Verizon Landline Discontinuance by State

State-by-state status of AT&T and Verizon applications to discontinue copper landline (POTS) service in the United States, with every logged carrier filing, FCC public notice, docket, automatic grant date, decision and earliest discontinuance date. Also includes AT&T copper retirement notices per year (2019 to 2025) and a dated log of developments. Every row links to its source.

**Data as of:** October 24, 2026

## Headline figures (as of October 24, 2026)

- 22 states have a logged FCC approval for AT&T or Verizon to discontinue copper landline service in parts of the state: AT&T in 19 (its 18-state batch plus California) and Verizon in 8, with 5 states on both lists.
- 36 states plus Washington, D.C. have an approval or a pending request.
- AT&T's 18-state batch can start on November 15, 2026.
- AT&T issued 494 copper retirement notices in 2025, up from 240 in 2024 (industry tally).

State counts are Metro Colo Advisory calculations from the filings in the dataset.

## Limits

- FCC approvals cover specific wire centers, not every line in a state. A carrier's notice letter decides whether a site is affected.
- A state with nothing logged is not safe by default. Lumen, Fidium and other carriers publish their own notices.
- AT&T's business-line application under FCC public notice DA 26-1075 covers 34 states and D.C., but the sources reviewed do not list the states, so it is not mapped by state.
- AT&T notices per year are a MetTel tally reported by DataRemote, not an FCC count.
