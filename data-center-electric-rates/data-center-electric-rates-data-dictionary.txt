DATA CENTER ELECTRIC RATES BY UTILITY: DATA DICTIONARY
Metro Colo Advisory, data as of October 29, 2026. License: CC BY 4.0.

File: data-center-electric-rates-by-utility.csv (one row per filing or proceeding, 105 rows)

COLUMNS

row_id
  Stable row ID (R001 onward). Gaps are rows removed after checking.

state
  Two-letter state code, or "Multi" for multistate filings.

utility
  Utility or utilities covered by the filing.

parent_company
  Parent company, where there is one.

filing_name
  Name or short description of the filing.

filing_type
  Kind of filing: large-load tariff, special contract, general rate case with a large-load class, rulemaking, generic proceeding, legislation.

commission
  Regulator or body handling the filing.

docket_number
  Docket, case or cause number as published.

docket_url
  Link to the docket or the best available source for it.

status
  Status as of the update date, in plain words.

filed_date
  Date filed (YYYY-MM-DD or YYYY-MM), where known.

decision_date
  Date decided, where decided.

next_date
  Next scheduled vote, deadline or effective date.

next_date_event
  What happens on next_date.

residential_bill_impact_as_stated
  Household bill figure exactly as stated, with who stated it. Not calculated by us.

bill_impact_source_url
  Source for the bill figure.

customer_protections
  Terms that keep large-customer costs with the large customer: thresholds, minimum payments, contract length, collateral, exit fees.

dc_load_figure_mw
  Data center or large-load figure in MW as reported (GW x 1,000). Not comparable across utilities without reading dc_load_term_used.

dc_load_term_used
  The utility's own term for the figure (signed, pipeline, queue, forecast).

dc_load_as_of
  Date of the load figure.

dc_load_source_url
  Source for the load figure.

system_peak_mw
  Utility system peak in MW, where sourced.

system_peak_year
  Year of the peak.

system_peak_source_url
  Source for the peak.

notes
  Context, caveats and corrections.

confidence
  high = confirmed in a primary document; medium = confirmed in secondary coverage only.

LIMITS OF THE DATA
- Bill impacts are copied exactly as stated by the utility, regulator or news source named in bill_impact_source_url. They are not calculated by Metro Colo Advisory and often cover a whole rate case, not the data center portion alone.
- Data center load figures use each utility's own term (signed, pipeline, queue, forecast). They are not comparable across utilities without reading dc_load_term_used.
- System peaks are for the utility's whole system where that is how the source reports them, which can span several states.
- Rows marked confidence = medium are confirmed in secondary coverage only, not yet in a primary filing.
- Status reflects the update date. Check docket_url for the latest.
- Gaps in row_id are rows removed after checking.
