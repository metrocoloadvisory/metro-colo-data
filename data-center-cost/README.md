# Data Center Cost 2026: Build, Lease, Run and AI Hardware Figures

Every figure on Metro Colo Advisory's Data Center Cost 2026 page, as downloadable data: what it costs to build, lease and run a data center in North America, AI hardware prices, market capacity and timelines. Each row links to its published source; Metro Colo Advisory calculations are marked.

**Data as of:** October 24, 2026
**Publisher:** [Metro Colo Advisory](https://metrocoloadvisory.com/)
**Page:** https://metrocoloadvisory.com/data-center-cost/
**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.23652508](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23652508)
**License:** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

## Headline figures

- $17.6 million per MW to build all in, US and Canada, excluding chips (Cushman & Wakefield, 2026), up 21% since late 2024.
- $12.08 per watt construction-only US average; $14 to $16 for AI-specific facilities (Turner & Townsend 2026-27).
- About $204 per kW per month to lease 250 to 500 kW in primary markets, mid-2026 (Metro Colo Advisory calculation from CBRE).
- 1.4% vacancy in primary North American markets, H1 2026 (CBRE).
- Average PUE 1.52 (Uptime Institute, 2026); about $1.22 million a year to power 1 MW of IT at 9.17 cents per kWh (calculation from EIA).

## Limits

Published averages and ranges, not quotes. All-in development cost and construction-only cost measure different things. GPU and hardware prices move quickly; check the period column.
